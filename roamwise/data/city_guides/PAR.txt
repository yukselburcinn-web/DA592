In Paris, 166 of this catalogue's 400 stops sit in the central cluster around Notre-Dame de Paris, most of them within 1.4 km of its centre and comfortably walkable end to end. Bibliothèque nationale de France and Eiffel Tower anchor clusters 3-4 km out, each better treated as its own half-day than as a detour. 46 of the 82 food and nightlife stops fall inside that same central cluster, so evenings need no separate trip. The catalogue leans landmark, culture and museum, and about 31% of it is free to enter. Published closing times are known for 148 of these stops and run to about 18:00, so the indoor half of a day is best taken before then. Note the weekly closures when sequencing indoor stops: the Louvre Museum shuts on Tuesdays and the Musée d'Orsay on Mondays, and much of the independent food scene closes for August.
