In Berlin, 119 of this catalogue's 283 stops sit in the central cluster around Pergamon Museum, most of them within 1.6 km of its centre and comfortably walkable end to end. Brandenburg Gate, Berlin Wall and Berlin Zoological Garden anchor clusters 2-5 km out, each better treated as its own half-day than as a detour. 23 of the 31 food and nightlife stops fall inside that same central cluster, so evenings need no separate trip. The catalogue leans landmark, museum and culture, and about 34% of it is free to enter. Published closing times are known for 83 of these stops and run to about 18:00, so the indoor half of a day is best taken before then. Shops across Germany stay shut on Sundays, the Museum Island houses close on Mondays, and clubs here do not fill until well after midnight, so an evening planned around 21:00 will run early.
