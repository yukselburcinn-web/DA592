Prague's Old Town, Charles Bridge and Prague Castle form a single scenic corridor across the Vltava, walkable in a day but best spread over two to linger. The castle complex is large enough to fill a half-day on its own, including St. Vitus Cathedral. Josefov, the historic Jewish Quarter, sits inside the Old Town loop. Prague remains one of the most budget-friendly major European capitals, with cheap beer and affordable museum tickets relative to Western Europe. Crowds peak in summer and around the Christmas markets; a dawn walk across Charles Bridge before the tour groups arrive is a favorite local tip.
